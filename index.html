Formula: patients times the patient value basis, times the collection rate, times 12. Patient value is billed allowable, not cash; the collection rate converts it. Projections are estimates based on stated assumptions and do not guarantee results.
Methodology. Source: Brightree exports, "Wound Raw Data_Confirm" (confirmed or billed orders, Jan 2025 to Aug 5, 2026) and the wound open invoice receivables export, pulled Aug 6, 2026. Orders are unique SO numbers; patients are unique patient names per month; patient months are the sum of monthly unique patients. The real collection rate is measured only on mature months, those with at least 90 days of collection runway (through Apr 2026). Write off fields are unpopulated in Brightree exports, so open balance is computed as allowable less collections. August 2026 is partial. This dashboard is a static snapshot; regenerate from fresh exports to update.
