Methodology and caveats. Source: Brightree exports, "Wound Raw Data_Confirm" (confirmed or billed orders, Jan 2025 to Aug 5, 2026) and the wound open invoice receivables export, pulled Aug 6, 2026. Orders are unique SO numbers; patients are unique patient names per period. Patient value is billed allowable per unique patient per month, not cash. The real collection rate is measured only on months with at least 90 days of collection runway (May 2025 to Apr 2026, 74.4%); rates shown for recent months are not final. Partial August 2026 is excluded from all trends. Margin is allowable less COGS as exported. Write off fields are unpopulated in Brightree exports, so open balance is computed as allowable less collections. This dashboard is a static snapshot; regenerate from fresh exports to update.
